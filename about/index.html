About Us

Our Story

Guardian Group Safety & Leadership Solutions was founded by Haley and David Vincent, two industry professionals with a shared passion for ensuring work is done efficiently and safely. After years of hands-on experience in tree care, aviation, and leadership positions, they saw a gap in the way safety was taught and created Guardian Group to try to help change that.

Guardian Group Safety & Leadership Solutions helps organizations build stronger operations by focusing on the leadership, systems, and supporting the people doing the work. We don’t treat safety as a separate function or something that can be managed independently from day-to-day operations. We believe safe work is the natural result of operations that are well designed, well supported, and backed by strong leadership. Our approach looks beyond traditional safety programs to understand realistically how work actually gets done. By strengthening operational systems, developing supportive leadership, and giving teams the training and support they need to succeed, we help organizations create environments where safety isn’t a separate entity, it’s built into the way the work is done.

Our Philosophy

At Guardian Group, we believe that, contrary to popular belief, you don’t need a “safety cop,” and that policing safety can actually make your operation less safe in the grand scheme of things.

When people are blamed and punished for making an unsafe choice, they don’t necessarily stop making those choices. They get better at hiding them. That leaves leaders addressing the behavior they can see without ever understanding what drove that decision in the first place.

We can’t control every decision a person makes. We can control the system we ask them to work within.

Because safety isn’t something you police into an organization. It’s the result of an operation that works well.

How We Work

Instead of asking, “Why did this person make an unsafe choice?” we ask, “What made that choice make sense in the moment?”

Was there production pressure? An inefficient process? Inadequate training? Conflicting expectations? A missing resource? A system that made the safer option harder, slower, or less practical?

We focus on understanding how the work actually gets done and identifying the conditions that influence behavior. Then, we work alongside leaders to address the systems behind recurring problems and develop practical solutions that make work safer, more efficient, and more effective — building operations where the safe choice is also the practical choice.

Our Values

  • Top-Down Leadership. Leaders create the conditions their teams work within. Meaningful change starts with leadership taking ownership of those conditions.
  • People-First. People are not the problem. We seek to understand their experience, respect their expertise, and build systems that support them in doing their work safely & efficiently.
  • Accountability. Accountability means owning what is within your control. We help organizations move beyond blame and focus on what leaders, teams, and systems can actually change.
  • Practicality. A solution only works if it works in the real world. We prioritize straightforward, sustainable improvements that make sense for the people expected to use them.
  • Objectivity. We approach problems with curiosity instead of assumptions, looking at what is actually happening before deciding what should change.
  • Humility. Good leadership requires the willingness to question your own assumptions, hear uncomfortable information, and change course when the evidence says there’s a better way.
