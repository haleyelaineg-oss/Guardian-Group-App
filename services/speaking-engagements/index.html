Speaking Engagements

Thought-provoking sessions that challenge familiar assumptions and give audiences a more practical way to think about safety, leadership, and performance.

Guardian Group brings real experience from high-risk operations into honest, engaging conversations about why people make the decisions they do and what leaders can change to improve outcomes. Our sessions connect human factors, operational systems, and leadership behavior without reducing complex problems to slogans.

We speak for conferences, association events, leadership meetings, and company programs. Each session is shaped around the audience and event goals, with practical ideas participants can recognize in their own work and use when they return to it.

Topics

Ideas worth taking back to work

Safety Through Operations

Why many “safety problems” are operational problems with unsafe results—and what leaders can do about them.

Human Factors & Decisions

A practical look at the pressures, tradeoffs, and system conditions that make a choice feel reasonable in the moment.

Leadership Under Pressure

How clear priorities, trust, preparation, and communication shape performance when the stakes are high.
